# 10 real questions my user will ask

Write them the way your user would type them. At least 3 must need more than one page to answer, and at least 1 must be a question your documents cannot answer.

| # | Question | Where the answer is (document, page) | Needs more than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How much of the issue is a fresh issue versus offer for sale, and what is the price band? | Cover page / Issue Structure section (typically pages 1–5 of any DRHP) | No |
| 2 | What are the top five risk factors specific to this company's business? | Risk Factors section (typically 30–70+ pages in a DRHP) | Yes |
| 3 | How will the company use the proceeds from the fresh issue, and what is the estimated deployment timeline? | Objects of the Issue section (typically 5–15 pages in a DRHP) | Yes |
| 4 | Who are the promoters, how much of the company do they currently own, and how much are they selling through the offer for sale? | Promoters and Promoter Group / Capital Structure sections (typically 10–20 pages) | Yes |
| 5 | What outstanding litigation has the company or its promoters disclosed, and what is the total amount involved? | Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments section (typically 20–60 pages in a DRHP) | Yes |
| 6 | How have the company's revenue, EBITDA, and net profit changed over the last three audited financial years? | Financial Statements / Financial Information section (typically 50–120 pages) | Yes |
| 7 | What are the key industry tailwinds and what market size estimates does the prospectus cite? | Industry Overview section (typically 20–50 pages, often citing CRISIL or ICRA reports) | Yes |
| 8 | What is the company's debt position and what are the key terms of its material borrowings? | Financial Statements / Material Contracts section (typically 10–30 pages) | No |
| 9 | How does the company's post-issue P/E valuation compare with the listed peers disclosed in the prospectus? | Basis for Issue Price section (typically 3–8 pages) | No |
| 10 | What is the current secondary-market trading price of the company's shares today? | Cannot be answered from the documents — DRHPs are pre-listing filings and contain no live market price data | No |
